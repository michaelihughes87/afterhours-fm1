# Initial factory preset bank

These are target names and sound briefs, not completed presets.

## Drums
1. **Rain on Concrete** — dusty, swung break feel.
2. **Basement Kit** — dry kick/snare with restrained room.
3. **Wire & Glass** — metallic industrial percussion.
4. **Sunday Dub** — sparse one-drop / dub accents.
5. **Last Train** — muted hats, papery snare, low kick.

## Bass
1. **Subway** — rounded sine/triangle sub.
2. **Blackline** — dark, driven mono bass.
3. **Rubber Room** — elastic, slightly unstable bass.
4. **Cold Current** — restrained FM/digital bass.

## Synths
1. **Blue Hour** — slow chorus pad.
2. **Fluorescent** — hollow, detuned pulse.
3. **Afterimage** — dark sustained minor chord.
4. **Wire Choir** — metallic, dissonant FM texture.
5. **Empty Platform** — sparse pluck with dark delay.

## Textures
1. **Dust Archive** — filtered hiss and crackle.
2. **Room Tone 03** — quiet, evolving noise bed.
3. **Tape Ghost** — pitch-worn atmospheric layer.

## Quality bar

Every preset must include:
- A clear musical role and useful playable range.
- Sensible default volume relative to other presets.
- No clipping or harsh transient surprises.
- A short description and suggested use.
- A clean, editable starting point rather than excessive hidden modulation.
